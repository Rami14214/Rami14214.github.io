Live client projectPythonFastAPIPostgreSQLSupabaseGSAPRender

The studio took every booking through Instagram messages. I designed and built a site where clients see the real menu, pick a staff member and a free time, and book in under a minute. Staff run it all from a private dashboard.

95automated tests
